# ScrbL — Third-Party Font & Artwork Notices

This application (`ScrbL`) operates 100% locally offline and bundles the following open-source font families for its user-selectable typography options (Serif, Clean Sans, and Handwritten), alongside original hand-drawn doodle SVG/VectorDrawable artwork created specifically for ScrbL.
